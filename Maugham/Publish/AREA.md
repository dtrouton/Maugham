# Publish — Area guide

PDF and EPUB publishing through the bundled tectonic. What the body emitter produces is `Maugham/Resources/PublishStarter/EMISSION.md`, generated from `EmissionContract.swift`. This guide began as the text CLAUDE.md's area table carried for this directory.

## From CLAUDE.md's area table (2026-10-17)

- PDF+EPUB via bundled tectonic. Flow: `ProjectStoreASTSource` → `ProjectASTBuilder` → `LaTeXBodyEmitter`/`XHTMLBodyEmitter` → output under `Exports/`. `EMISSION.md` is generated (edit `EmissionContract.swift`, run tests). Per-piece `style_file` must stay inside a scoped `\begingroup…\endgroup` — don't hoist the `\input` out. ADR 0013.
- Every compile — a fresh version, a language edition, or a republish — mints its version and its `-r<suffix>` suffix (when republishing) up front, before compile, through an effective config; the filename, the artifact-internal stamp, and the catalog row all agree, and republish can never wear the original's filename.
- Same-edition concurrent compiles are refused fast by `PublishMintGate` (a per-project actor in `PublishingStores`) rather than raced — a refusal, not a queue: nothing waits its turn, and a `dry_run` is exempt because it mutates nothing (issue #25).
- **The publish department (M4-scale, shipped 2026-08-20) gives Publish its own desk** — `DetailSegment.department` (⌘⌥K), leading the persona — where a named **translator per language** (`ProductionRole`, presets Cortázar/Baudelaire/Tieck/Motoyuki/Kiš (es/fr/de/ja/sr; readers and collators alongside), `Packages/MaughamCore/Sources/MaughamCore/ProductionRole.swift`) and a named **designer** (`Tschichold`) run confined `claude -p` sessions on the compiler's own rails (`TranslatorOrchestrator`/`DesignerOrchestrator`, third and fourth conformers over `CompilerOrchestrator`'s shape — see `Maugham/Compiler/AREA.md`). A translation Run resolves against the OPEN document only and writes through the same `TranslationWritePipeline` `write_translation` uses; a design Run proposes a spec plus complete templates plus compiled sample pages, staged under `.maugham/design/proposals/` and never touching the live publish tree until the writer judges it at the **gate** (`.designProposal`, a third arm of the one `publishCentre` switch) with one of four verbs — Approve / Request Changes / Revert / Finalize — each refusing in its own words, Finalize being the one irreversible step (no `NSUndoManager` at any depth). Answering a translator's query can **Answer as ruling…** in the same act as the reply, filing a dated entry under that edition's own **brief** (`Statement.Kind.editionBrief`, a third statement kind beside intent and visual language, hosted through the same `StatementPane`).
- **The desk starts editions and names the cast as of cast-management (2026-08-21)**: a stored `.translator` role is a third source of `EditionStatus`' language union beside translation files and open queries (so `translation_status` reports a role-only edition too — one derivation, never two answers), **Add Language…** mints that role named, and **Rename …** on every row is the first writer-facing caller `renameProductionRole` has had. All three are asks of ONE sheet (`DepartmentCastSheet`), so `translatorRole(for:)` + `renameProductionRole` is the only way anybody on this book is named.
- **Imprints P1 (2026-08-28) add a fourth identity dimension**: a project's `config.imprints` names special editions, each with its own template, rendered set, metadata, cover and version counter, and `PublishConfig.resolved(imprint:pieceIDs:)` is the ONE door — everything downstream (compilers, the filename builder, the coverage gate, the snapshot) reads a plain, already-resolved config off `config.template`/`config.imprint` and never takes imprint as an argument of its own (`TripwireGrepTests`' census). The mint key and `Publication` are four-part, `(imprint, version, language, format)`; `compile`/`preview_compile`/`list_publications`/`read_publication_page` all speak `imprint`, with the sentinel `"book"` selecting what no imprint compiled. See `docs/guide/publishing.md`'s Imprints section.
- **Imprints P2 (2026-08-28) lets one document hold more than one language's complete body**: `LanguageSet` reconciles the legacy `language` param, the new ordered `languages` list and the `"source"` sentinel into `bodies: [String?]` (`nil` = source), and `BodyPlan` turns that into one bound-and-folded `BodyPlan.Body` per tag that `PDFCompiler`/`EPUBCompiler` render in order under a `MaughamBody` wrapper (PDF) or per-body sectioned XHTML (EPUB); the joined identity (`LanguageSet.identity`, e.g. `"en+sr"`) is what a multi-body compile's `Publication`, filename and mint-gate reservation carry, while `LanguageSet.singleTag` is what a single-body compile still resolves templates and metadata against. See `docs/guide/publishing.md`'s Bilingual editions section.
- **Imprints P3 (2026-08-28) anchors every emitted paragraph and cross-links sluglines across bodies, and gives the desk its own compile**: `\hypertarget{p-<tag>-<¶id>}{}`/`id="p-<tag>-<¶id>"` on every node a paragraph produced (LaTeX/XHTML), `\MaughamCrossLink`/`<a href>` linking a slugline to the same slugline in each other rendered body, and two `\providecommand` fallbacks in every body's prologue so a hyperref-less preamble still compiles. The Publish desk (⌘⌥K) gained an **Imprint** picker (Book + each declared imprint, persisted per project) and **Compile…**, a sheet running the same orchestrator, mint gate and coverage gate as the MCP `compile` tool; every surface naming a compiled book (`PublishPreviewCentre.parts(for:)`, the desk's status line, the Exports footer) reads one shared composition, so none of them can disagree about what a book is called. See `docs/guide/publishing.md`'s Bilingual editions/Imprints sections and `docs/guide/publish-department.md`.
- **The translation pipeline (2026-08-29, P1–P4; [ADR 0030](../../docs/adr/0030-three-people-seven-legs-directives-as-rulings.md)) gives every language a blind reader and a collator beside its translator**, and the desk's Run now drives a seven-leg round (or the whole book, in binder order over the imprint's own document set) rather than one translator call — `Show` opens the **round report** in the centre column, `PublishCentre.translationRound`, a fourth arm of the same switch the design gate is a third arm of, written to the author in their own language with every verb pressable without reading the target one.
- A **directive** — the author's instruction about a paragraph of their English — is a ruling like any other and reaches a statement only through `RulingPerformer.rule`; the glossary is rulings of a recognised shape, drawn as a table.
- **Plan 5 (2026-09-02) built proposals into statements**: `propose_edition_brief`/`propose_visual_language` stage a draft under `.maugham/statements/proposals/` and the writer adopts or discards it at `StatementPane`'s gate with a diff (`StatementProposalGate.adopt` is the one write, a click; ADR 0030 §7 amended).
- The state machine, the briefings and the cold calls are `Maugham/Compiler/`'s; the surfaces are `Maugham/Views/`'s.
