# Going Further

You've seen the essentials. Here is the rest of the house.

## The whole book at once

Click the Book, the row at the top of the binder with this project's name. The centre shows the whole book, and the switch in the toolbar chooses how:

- Cards (⌃⌘1), one card for each chapter, with its line.
- Outline (⌃⌘2), the chapters as a table.
- Status (⌃⌘3), where each chapter stands, with its notes and to-dos still open. "Try It" counts the to-dos you haven't ticked yet.

Click a chapter in any of them to go back to writing it.

With the Book clicked, the right column is the whole book's too. To resolve (⌘1) lists what is waiting in every chapter, and Brief (⌘2) is where you say what the book itself is going for.

This chapter has a fold too. Look under its row in the binder: its fold shows that "Try It" links here.

## The canvas

Click Canvas in the binder for a place to think before anything has firmed up. Double-click anywhere on it to drop a scrap, and start typing. Most of what lands there will never become anything, and that is the point.

## Writing with Claude

Maugham is made to work beside Claude. Choose Help ▸ Set up Claude Desktop… to connect them. Claude can read your book, leave notes on particular paragraphs and do research for you. With Claude Code on your Mac, ⌘R (File ▸ Check Writing) asks for a reading of what you've written since the last check.

Claude's notes on your writing arrive in To resolve, beside your text and never in it. The manuscript is yours.

## Publishing

When a draft is ready, Maugham typesets it as a PDF or an EPUB. The finished files go in the project's `Exports/` folder.

## Help

Choose Help ▸ Maugham Help (⌘?) for the whole guide. Press ⌘/ for Syntax & Keyboard Shortcuts, a quick sheet of the marks you can type and the keys you can press.

When you're done exploring, delete this project like any other. It leaves nothing behind.
