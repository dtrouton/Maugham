# Going Further

You've seen the essentials. Here's where to go next.

## Help

Choose **Help → Maugham Help** for the full guide — focus chrome, the binder, research, history and time-travel, publishing, and every keyboard shortcut. Press ⌘/ at any time for the quick syntax and shortcut sheet.

## Writing with Claude

Maugham is built to live alongside Claude Desktop. Open Settings and follow the one-click setup to connect them. Claude can read your manuscript, leave notes against specific paragraphs, and help with research — all in a parallel annotation layer that never edits your prose behind your back.

## Publishing

When a draft is ready, Maugham can typeset it to a polished PDF or EPUB. The output lands in the project's `Exports/` folder. The bundled templates are a good starting point and can be tailored per piece.

When you're done exploring, delete this project like any other — it leaves nothing behind.
