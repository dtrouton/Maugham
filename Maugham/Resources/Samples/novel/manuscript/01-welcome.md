# Welcome to Maugham

This is a real project. Write in it, change anything, and delete it when you're done; it behaves exactly like one you make yourself.

Everything you type is saved as you go, as plain text on your own disk. There is no Save to remember. ⌘S still does something: it marks a checkpoint, a moment you can come back to. But your words were safe before you pressed it.

Try it now. Put your cursor at the end of this line and write a sentence of your own.

## Three columns

The window has three columns, and each has one job.

On the left is the binder, where you choose what the window is about. At the top is the Book, the whole of it. Below are the Canvas and these three chapters, and further down, Research. "Welcome" is the row tinted there, because it is the chapter open in the centre.

The centre is where the writing is. Right now it is this chapter.

On the right is a column with places you can turn to, one at a time. Press ⌘1 for To resolve: everything raised about this chapter that is waiting on you, a note from a reader, a to-do, a question. A chapter as new as this one has nothing yet, and says so. Press ⌘2 for Brief: what this chapter is meant to do, in your own words. This one already has its line, the sentence at the top of Brief.

The right column follows the binder. Click a chapter, and it is about that chapter. Click the Book, and it is about the whole book.

When you're ready, click "Try It" in the binder.
