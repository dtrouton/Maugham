# About the Research Pane

This is a research note. Research lives alongside your manuscript but is never published with it — it's for your own notes, character sheets, timelines, reference images, and links.

Drag a file in from the Finder, paste an image, or create a new text note. You can link a research note to a chapter so it's one click away while you write.

This sample's research is just this note. Delete it, edit it, or pile in your own — it's yours.
