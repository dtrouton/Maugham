# About This Sample

This is a research note, and it belongs to the whole book: it sits in the binder's Research section, under no chapter. Research is everything you keep beside the manuscript and never publish: character sheets, timelines, photographs, PDFs, web links, notes to yourself.

Make your own from the binder. Right-click Research for a new note, a table or a group, or drag a file in from the Finder.

A note about one chapter can live in that chapter's fold instead. Right-click a note and choose Move to Chapter to give it to one; Make Whole-Book brings a chapter's note back here.

Keep this note, delete it, or bury it under your own. It's yours.
