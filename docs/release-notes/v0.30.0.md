# Maugham 0.30.0

_Released 2026-08-19_

The loop closes, and your editors get names. Since 0.29 the compiler and
the review passes have stopped being two separate machines: what a check
raises now lands where you work it, each pass reads with its own editor's
eye, and the whole round-by-round loop — run, triage, run again — lives
in one place.

## What you'll notice

- **Your passes have editors.** Structural is read by **Perkins**, Line
  by **Lish**, Copyedit by **Gould**, Proof by **Argus** — each with an
  editorial brief that tells the model what that pass attends to and
  what it leaves alone (Gould queries an odd spelling in an experimental
  piece rather than correcting your voice; Perkins never flags a typo).
  Rename them per project; a custom pass reads under its own name.
- **A check's notes go to your queue.** Continuity questions and reader
  reports arrive as annotations — authored by the pass's editor, stamped
  with the pass, triagable, declinable, bulk-workable — instead of
  living and dying in the diagnostics pane. Decline one with a reason
  and the next round is told not to raise it again.
- **Author keeps its wet ink.** The diagnostics pane's new **This
  check** section shows what the run just raised, right there, in
  manuscript order — **Got it** settles a note, **Not this** declines
  it, both one gesture, both undoable with ⌘Z, and both visible to
  Review and the next briefing.
- **Review has a cockpit.** The queue opens with the working lane —
  *"Copyedit · Gould · round 2"* — and the lane line is itself the
  picker: click it to change passes. **Run round** and **Fresh Eyes**
  buttons sit beside it, "Checking N paragraphs…" shows while it runs,
  **Cancel** is right there, and afterwards the line reads *"Since
  round 1: 2 resolved · 1 persisting · 1 new."* A board chip's context
  menu can start the editor's round directly.
- **A check that dies says so** — in the strip that launched it, not
  just in Author — and checks get five minutes now instead of two, so a
  whole-piece first read finishes instead of timing out.
- **The nudge grew verbs.** "Structural still open on this piece" now
  offers **Mark done** and **Skip** in place, so closing a pass no
  longer means a trip to the Inspector. The verdict is still always
  yours — running a clean round never marks anything done for you.
- **Accepting any note is now undoable.** ⌘Z after accepting a comment
  or query reopens it — previously only suggestions could be unwound,
  and ⌘Z would silently eat your last edit instead.
- **Claude Desktop reads your doctrine.** `get_outline` serves each
  pass's brief, and the editing-pass skill now reviews at whatever
  altitude your own ladder names instead of its own hardcoded registers.

## Fixes

- Swapping passes at certain window sizes no longer pushes the binder
  tree's top rows out of view.
- A queue where you've settled every note reads as ready for the next
  round, not as "notes hidden by your filters."
- Bulk-accepting a mixed batch keeps every note's undo reachable
  (suggestions are applied first), and one ⌘Z reverses the batch.

## Known issues

- Pass briefs and editor names are preset-seeded; there's no in-app
  editor for them yet.
- Round memory is per document and six checks deep — a pass revisited
  after six intervening checks starts its numbering over.
- The phone reads everything this release writes but has no pass, queue,
  or editor surface of its own.
