# Maugham 0.32.0

_Released 2026-08-26_

The References shelf tells the truth about what a piece is pinned to, and
studying a reference no longer costs you the column you write in. Four
things a writer saw in one afternoon, fixed as one milestone.

## What you'll notice

- **References is complete for every kind of project.** A Collection
  piece's own research folder — the note a promoted canvas region became —
  and a short story's or screenplay's research now appear on the shelf
  (⌘⌥E) and in the compiler's briefing. Before, only a Novel chapter's
  *linked* research counted, so a Collection's shelf could show a region's
  scraps and not the note they had been promoted into.
- **Pins are grouped by the region they came from,** in the order you
  arranged them on the canvas, under the region's own name — not one
  alphabetical run. A promoted region shows **the note it became** rather
  than its cards; a card you bound to the piece by hand sits under
  **Cards**. The compiler is briefed on the same grouping.
- **Studying a pin opens in the right column,** in place of the pane
  picker, at the right column's width — not as a fourth column between
  the binder and your prose. Clicking a pin reveals the column if it was
  hidden. ✕, Escape, switching pane, choosing another subject, or pressing
  the pane's own shortcut (⌘⌥E brings the shelf back) closes it; switching
  persona, ⌘⌥I, or ⌘\ only hide it and it is still there when you return.
- **A research note keeps its line breaks.** Lines you typed inside a
  scrap or a note used to run together in the preview; they render as
  lines now, everywhere a note is read.
- **⌘\ on the planning canvas gives the canvas the whole window.** The
  right column used to go blank and take half the width; the same fault
  narrowed the editor slightly whenever the inspector was hidden in Author.
- Claude's **Show** on an arrival banner now opens the canvas Inspector it
  names, rather than whichever pane happened to be up.

## Under the hood

- No manifest schema change — Mac-only release; MaughamPhone 0.10.0 stays
  current.
- `PinnedReferences.pinned` returns a sectioned `PinnedShelf` and takes the
  piece's *contained* research as a required input; one assembler, three
  readers (shelf, study column, briefing). The briefing's pinned listing has
  a named ceiling with an "…and N more" line.
- The per-project `assistantColumnWidth` ui-state key is retired; old files
  still open.
- Two Publish-department gate tests read the accessibility tree before the
  render that publishes the verb's sentence and were red in the parallel
  suite only; they now wait for the sentence they assert.

## Known issues

- A research note hard-wrapped at a fixed column now previews with those
  wraps rather than reflowed — deliberate, since a scrap's line breaks are
  the point, but worth knowing if you wrap notes by hand.
