# Maugham 0.11.0

_Released 2026-06-10_

A speed release. 0.10.0 made typing in long documents feel like typing in
short ones; 0.11.0 finishes the job — the editor's whole keystroke path was
rebuilt and measured until it stopped showing up in a profiler at all.

## What's new

- **Typing in very long manuscripts is now effectively instant.** The
  screenplay parser and the paragraph engine were rewritten as single-pass
  scanners; the per-keystroke cost of a 120-page screenplay dropped about
  4× on top of 0.10.0's gains, and even a 250-page single file stays
  comfortably under the threshold of perception. Every rewrite was verified
  line-for-line against the previous engine across thousands of generated
  scripts — classification, dual dialogue, page counts, and styling are
  byte-identical, just faster.
- **The word/page-count footer and the Scenes sidebar no longer cost you
  keystrokes.** Their numbers are now derived from work the editor already
  did, computed just after you pause — never re-reading the whole document
  while you type.
- **The element gutter (SCENE/CHAR/DLG labels) only draws what's on
  screen.** Scrolling a huge script is smoother, and labels recolor
  instantly on theme changes.

## Fixes

- **The footer could briefly show the previous document's word count** when
  switching documents quickly. It now updates immediately on switch.

## Notes

- Sealing, history, sync, backups, publishing, and the iPhone companion are
  unchanged from 0.10.0.
- If you write 350-page single-file epics, there remains a barely-there
  keystroke cost at that extreme; it's understood, measured, and queued —
  and it does not affect documents at any realistic length.
