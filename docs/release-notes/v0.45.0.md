# Maugham 0.45.0

_Released 2026-11-11_

Faster typing in long documents, and version history for Research notes and palette cards. Mac only; MaughamPhone 0.16.0 is still current.

## What's new

- **Typing in a long document is quicker.** Maugham no longer counts the whole document's words on every key, and autosave runs in the background. In a 250 KB piece a keystroke takes about a third less time. The footer's word counts now settle a moment after you pause.
- **Research notes keep earlier versions.** A note's inspector has a Versions section, as a Research table's does. Restore brings an earlier version back, and restoring again returns the newer text.
  - Your own typing keeps one version per stretch of editing: the text as it was before you started. A pause of more than ten minutes starts a new stretch.
  - Anything else that changes a note always keeps what it replaces: Replace All, a renamed link, a canvas Rewrite, and Claude.
  - Each device keeps the 50 most recent versions of a note. This is lighter than a manuscript's history, which keeps every change.
- **Palette cards keep versions too.** Select a card in the tree to see its Versions in the inspector.
- **Claude can rewrite a Research note.** The new `update_note` tool replaces a note's text. The version it replaced is always kept and marked as Claude's change, so Restore brings your text back. Claude still cannot change manuscript text or palette cards this way.
- **A canvas Rewrite keeps a version instead of a Trash entry.** The text a Rewrite replaces is in the note's or card's Versions. If Maugham can't keep that version, the Rewrite doesn't happen and nothing changes. Entries earlier Rewrites left in the Trash still restore.

## Fixes

- **Trash**
  - The Trash at the foot of the sidebar shows its items when expanded. It was opening empty.
  - A Research note deleted from inside a piece restores into that piece's Research. If the piece is gone, it restores into the project's Research.
  - Deleting several Research items at once either deletes all of them or none.
- **Research notes**
  - A note open in the editor now shows text written to it from elsewhere, such as Replace All or a renamed link, without reopening.
  - If a note can't be saved as you leave it, your text is kept as a conflict backup and Maugham names the file.
  - A view-only share can no longer type into a Research note.
- **Palette cards**
  - Renaming a card that has pictures while still typing keeps its pictures.
- **Damaged projects**
  - Maugham refuses to write, rename or move a Research item whose entry points outside Research. Deleting such an item removes its entry and leaves the file alone.
- **Closing a document**
  - The last keystroke before closing a document is saved.

## Known issues

- Restoring a version of a note or card from before it was renamed brings back links to its old picture folder, so those pictures don't show.
- A palette card's Versions can't be reached from the palette wall; select the card in the tree.
