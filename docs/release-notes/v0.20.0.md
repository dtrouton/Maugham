# Maugham 0.20.0

_Released 2026-07-11_

The palette leaves the desk. Aim a photo, a voice note, or a line of text
at a palette subject from your phone the moment you encounter the thing —
then land it on the card from your Mac. And the palette's one known rough
edge is gone: rename your Palette folder or craft-intent doc to anything
you like; everything stays connected.

## What's new

- **Promote captures into palette cards.** The Inbox pane (⌘⌥6) can now
  send an entry straight onto a card: a photo joins the card's images, a
  voice note or text line becomes a sensory note (sense-tagged when it was
  tagged at capture). Captures aimed from the phone offer their card
  one click deep — **Promote to Palette: The Flat** — and a subject that
  matches no card yet offers to mint one.
- **Rename freely.** The palette folder and craft-intent docs now carry
  durable identity in the project manifest, so renaming them through the
  Research pane no longer detaches anything — the Palette segment even
  shows your chosen name. Existing projects heal automatically the first
  time they're opened; nothing to do.
- **Claude can promote into cards too.** `promote_inbox_entry` accepts a
  palette card (by id or subject name); `list_inbox` shows each capture's
  aim. Claude never mints cards on its own — that stays your call.
- Pairs with **MaughamPhone 0.6.0**, which adds palette capture-aiming and
  a read-only palette browser — see its notes.

## Fixes

- The v0.19.0 known issue ("renaming the Palette folder detaches the
  wall") is fixed by the durable-identity work above.

## Compatibility

- Update together with MaughamPhone 0.6.0 for the capture-aim flow. Older
  phone versions keep working — aimed fields are simply absent from their
  captures, and everything else is unchanged.
