# Maugham 0.12.1

_Released 2026-06-16_

A small follow-up to the onboarding release: Maugham now carries a license, and
the in-app help text reads the way it should.

## What's new

- **Maugham now has a license.** The source is released under the **PolyForm
  Noncommercial License 1.0.0** — you're free to read, build, and use it for
  personal and noncommercial purposes; commercial rights are reserved. See
  `LICENSE.md`.

- **Help → Acknowledgements.** A new window credits the bundled third-party
  components (WhisperKit, Tectonic) and reproduces their MIT licenses, so the
  required notices now travel with the app itself.

## Fixes

- **Help and Acknowledgements text wraps correctly.** Paragraphs in the in-app
  guide and the acknowledgements window were breaking at the source file's
  line wrapping instead of flowing to the window width. They now reflow as
  normal wrapped paragraphs.

## Notes

- No changes to your manuscripts, editing, sync, publishing, or the iPhone
  companion since 0.12.0.
