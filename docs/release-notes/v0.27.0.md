# Maugham 0.27.0

_Released 2026-08-09_

The biggest release Maugham has shipped. Since 0.26 the app has grown a
second axis: not just what you're writing, but what kind of attention
you're giving it. Four personas reshape the window around planning,
drafting, reviewing, and publishing; a freeform canvas holds the thinking
that comes before chapters; a compiler reads your manuscript against your
own stated intent every time you ask; and the left column becomes one
tree that names everything the project holds.

## What you'll notice

- **Four personas (⌘1–⌘4).** Plan, Author, Review, Publish — each
  reconfigures the window's three columns around one stage of the work.
  Nothing is ever locked away: personas are lenses, not gates.
- **The planning canvas.** Plan's centre is a spatial surface for
  before-it's-firm thinking: scraps you type, regions that gather them,
  lines that connect them, pictures dropped straight from Finder or a
  browser, captures sent from your phone's inbox. Anything worth keeping
  promotes into real research or palette artifacts (⌘⇧↩) — with a mark
  left behind saying where it went. Claude can read the whole board and
  add scraps of its own; its cards sit perfectly straight and slightly
  cooler, so *straight means Claude* at a glance.
- **Intent, and a compiler that reads against it.** Every piece (and the
  book) can carry a statement of intent — what this chapter is doing, in
  your own prose. ⌘R sends what changed since the last run to Claude and
  the Diagnostics pane (⌘⌥D) answers in your terms: which of your own
  clauses hold, which strain, what a fresh reader felt, what facts the
  book has quietly established. Answering a note turns it into a dated
  ruling the next run is briefed on. Notes worth keeping promote to
  tasks. A piece it has never read gets an honest offer to read it whole
  first — and streams its report as it works.
- **One tree on the left, in every persona.** Pieces (each unfolding to
  its own research), Research, Palette — one column, one selection, one
  set of rules. The segment strip is gone. Find (⌘⌥F) is an overlay of
  the tree; Trash is a disclosure at its foot; multi-select works
  (⌘-click, then batch move, drag, or delete); files, images, folders,
  and pasted URLs land on the tree exactly where you drop them —
  a Finder file on a chapter links it, on a collection piece it moves in.
  The palette wall keeps a door ("Open Wall" on the Palette header).
- **The right column holds one width** you set once, through every
  persona and pane switch.
- **Publishing is stricter about identity.** A compile refuses to
  overwrite an occupied destination; republish can never wear the
  original's filename; same-edition concurrent compiles are refused
  rather than raced.
- **Trash says what came back.** "Restore Last Deletion" (⌘⌥Z) and the
  trash's own Restore both name anything a restore could not give back,
  the moment it happens.

## Under the hood

- Statements (intent, visual language) are op-logged documents at the
  project root — plain text, synced, undoable — and the compiler is
  read-only by construction: confinement is enforced flags plus a census;
  the one door back into your layer is your own rulings.
- The behavioural register: `register/` now carries 400+ verified claims
  about what the code does, each pinned by a test, and 54 rulings —
  Denver's case law — reconciled against the constitution. Strict reads
  landed for the op log: an unreadable file refuses loudly at the door
  instead of shrugging into empty state.
- Canvas sidecar at schema 9 (multi-valued contribution records); project
  manifest at schema 5; the reserved "Palette" group name refuses at
  creation and rename instead of colliding with the palette folder.
- MCP grew to 55+ tools including `list_canvas`, `add_canvas_scraps`,
  `read_visual_language`, and piece-reference projections — Claude's
  reads all derive from the op log, never the `.md` on disk.

## Fixes

- The collection tree indents fold children under their parent instead of
  a hair to its left.
- A pane whose text demanded more height than the window could ever give
  no longer drags all three columns past the window's edge (the
  cold-start offer and the view-only notice both did this; a census now
  refuses any pane that tries).
- Research subjects arriving in Plan reveal the column that shows them —
  clicking a note, or Claude's "Show", always lands somewhere visible.
- ⌘V of a URL into research works (it was silently dropped before).
- A restored legacy window can no longer reopen into surfaces that no
  longer exist.

## Known issues

- The compiler's cold-start read of a very long piece can take a couple
  of minutes; the report streams as it arrives.
- A research note linked to two chapters refuses an ambiguous drag to the
  shared section — unlink from the Links inspector instead.
