# Maugham 0.28.0

_Released 2026-08-13_

The shell finish lands whole, and the manuscript learns to survive damage.
Since 0.27 the window's centre column has learned to render whatever the
tree names — a corkboard at project altitude, the compiled book in
Publish, a lit card on Plan's board — and beneath all of it the op log
gained a recovery ladder: a manuscript file that can't be read no longer
stops the writing.

## What you'll notice

- **The project row means *zoom out*.** Selecting the project (or a
  group) in Author, Review, or Publish fills the centre with the
  corkboard/outline — cards ↔ table toggle in its header — and clicking a
  card opens that chapter in place. The centre never renders nothing.
  ⌘⌥O selects the project row; ⌘⌥R/⌘⌥P open the tree's Research and
  Palette sections and scroll them into view.
- **Plan travels on a double-click.** Double-click any tree row in Plan —
  a chapter, the project row, a research note, a palette card — and you
  land in Author with that thing open. One gesture; the subject carries.
  Open Wall in Plan does the same: it opens the wall in Author, where
  there's a column to spare.
- **Publish shows the book.** With a compiled PDF, the Publish persona's
  centre is the book itself at project level, with a picker in its header
  to flip between your compiled editions (newest first; a fresh compile
  snaps back to it). A chapter still opens the editor — tweak for layout,
  then ⌘⌥O back to the book. Before anything is compiled you get the
  corkboard with an honest "no compiled book yet" notice; a publications
  catalog that exists but can't be read says so *by name* — unreadable
  never masquerades as empty.
- **Plan's board lights your research.** Click a research note or palette
  card in Plan's tree and its card on the canvas lights while the rest of
  the board recedes; if it sits off-screen the camera brings it to you.
  An item with no card yet says so, and tells you the drag that places it.
- **Review adjudicates.** Research notes and palette cards open read-only
  in Review's centre and on its wall — selectable, scrollable, not
  editable. Editing is one ⌘ away in any other persona; the tree's own
  verbs (rename, create, delete) work everywhere.
- **Link Research… is back, with a keyboard.** A chapter row's context
  menu offers the searchable link picker again — toggle research onto a
  chapter without the drag. The chapter's fold updates live.
- **Reveals actually reveal.** A Claude "Show", a find match, or ⌘⌥R now
  opens whatever it takes for the row to be on screen — including a
  Collection piece's own research fold — and scrolls the tree to it.
- **The manuscript survives damage.** If a document's op log can't be
  fully read, Maugham no longer refuses the project: it loads what it
  can, tells you exactly what it couldn't (by file, with the reason),
  and keeps the words safe. Damaged history can be **set aside and
  writing continues**; the set-aside history stays visible in the History
  pane, retries itself on every open, and merges back honestly — verified,
  by the sync rules, never overwriting. ⌘S's flash now fires only when a
  checkpoint actually landed.
- **Unreadable files name themselves everywhere.** Checkpoints,
  publications, pending edits, backups: a file that exists but can't be
  read is reported with its reason, everywhere one used to read as
  silently absent — and backups refuse to run when the manuscript's own
  integrity is in question.

## Fixes

- A flicked canvas card now glides by the gesture's own timing — the
  flick's age no longer includes our processing time, so fast flicks land
  where your hand sent them (#34).
- Canvas robustness sweep (#28): orphaned scraps resurrect as loose cards
  at load; a refused sidecar survives being looked at; thumbnail decode
  is pre-gated on claimed dimensions; content writes land before the
  derived sidecar.
- Capture and statement ingest failure paths (#29): a failed image ingest
  leaves nothing behind; a statement mint that neither bound nor
  deposited rolls back; inbox image retry converges.
- The compiler's derivation deadline answers on its own authority.

## Known issues

- A device can return set-aside history that another device deliberately
  set aside (the archive syncs; the return is the diagnostic — by design
  for now, recorded as a ruling).
- On the canvas, VoiceOver can hear a line but still has no route to
  select one without the mouse.
