# Maugham 0.5.0

_Released 2026-05-29_

Groundwork for the forthcoming **MaughamPhone** companion. Most of what's here is
dormant until the phone app ships — it lands now so the Mac side is ready.

## What's new
- **Inbox pane (⌘⌥6).** A triage surface for captures — text, photos, and voice
  notes — that will sync in from MaughamPhone. Promote a capture into your
  research, edit a voice note's transcript, play it back, or trash it (with a
  Trash view to undo). Until the companion app ships, the inbox stays empty.
- **Local voice transcription.** Voice captures are transcribed on-device with
  WhisperKit — no cloud, no API keys. Apple Silicon only; pick the model under
  **Settings → Voice**. The phone's quick on-device draft is replaced by the
  higher-quality transcript, and your manual edits are never overwritten.
- **Claude can help triage the inbox.** Three new MCP tools let Claude Desktop
  list inbox captures and promote them into your research folder (read + promote
  only — it can't add or delete).

## Fixes
- **Hardened multi-device sync.** The per-document operation log is now
  partitioned per device, so two machines editing the same project over iCloud
  Drive can no longer silently drop each other's writes as conflict copies.

## Known issues
- The **MaughamPhone** companion app isn't released yet, so the Inbox has nothing
  to show until it is.
- Local voice transcription requires an **Apple Silicon** Mac; on Intel the
  on-device draft from the phone is the only transcript.
