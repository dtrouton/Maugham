# Maugham 0.12.5

_Released 2026-06-30_

Your manuscript files on disk are now clean, standard Markdown and Fountain —
nothing but the words you wrote.

## What's new

- **Clean files on disk.** Maugham used to tuck small invisible markers into
  your `.md` and `.fountain` files (HTML comments it used to keep track of
  paragraphs and tasks). Those are gone from the file now. Your manuscripts on
  disk are plain, standard Markdown/Fountain — pleasant to read, clean in a
  `git diff`, and ready to hand to a collaborator, open in any other editor, or
  run through tools like pandoc. Nothing about your writing changes; only
  Maugham's internal bookkeeping leaves the file. (Maugham keeps that
  bookkeeping safely in its own records, so paragraph identity, history, tasks,
  and cross-device sync all keep working exactly as before.)

  This happens automatically and gradually: each file is cleaned the next time
  it's saved. Files you don't touch are left alone and keep working.

## Notes

- **Editing happens in Maugham.** As always, Maugham is the source of truth for
  your manuscripts. If you edit a manuscript file in another app while Maugham
  has it open, Maugham keeps its own up-to-date version — and now does so
  quietly, without interrupting you with a conflict prompt. Your outside change
  is set aside as a safety copy under the project's `.maugham/conflicts/` folder,
  just in case. (Syncing your work between Macs is unaffected — that flows
  through Maugham's own records, not the file on disk.)
- The iPhone companion app is unchanged (it ships separately).
