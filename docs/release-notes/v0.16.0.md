# Maugham 0.16.0

_Released 2026-07-07_

Research that belongs to a piece now shows up where you're writing — no
re-linking, no hunting. The right pane's Research mode, the capture inbox,
and Claude all understand the same idea: a piece's research is *its*
research.

## What's new

- **Piece research appears automatically.** Open a collection piece and the
  right pane's Research mode (⌘⌥2) now shows its research folder's contents
  in a "Piece Research" section — the moment a note exists, it's there. No
  linking step. Short stories and screenplays get the same treatment as
  "Project Research". (The mode is now called **Research** — it holds more
  than links.)
- **Create research without leaving the manuscript.** The Research pane's
  **+** menu adds notes, files, and web links scoped to the document you're
  writing: into the piece's folder in a collection, auto-linked to the
  chapter in a novel.
- **Promote captures straight to a chapter or piece.** The inbox's
  right-click menu now offers three destinations: shared research, the
  document you have open, or any chapter/piece via a searchable picker. A
  phone capture can land as research for Chapter 3 in one step.
- **Claude sees piece research.** `promote_inbox_entry` accepts a target
  document, and the reference-graph tools (`list_all_links`,
  `find_references`) now report folder-scoped piece research alongside
  explicit links — so Claude's picture matches the panes.
- **Smarter linking.** The link picker no longer offers research a piece
  already owns, lists shared items first, and drag-linking skips items that
  are already in the derived section.

## Fixes

- **New chapters accept typing immediately.** Naming a freshly created
  chapter or piece could leave the editor silently ignoring every keystroke
  until you switched documents and back — the rename moved the file under
  the open editor. The editor now follows the file.
- Research promoted with a piece to a standalone project appears in the new
  project's Research pane with no re-linking (pinned by a regression test).
- The ⌘⌥2 mode is named "Research" consistently everywhere — menu bar,
  keyboard-shortcut reference, and Help — and the Help topics for the
  Inspector and Research pane now describe exactly what each shows.

## Known issues

- Linking a piece's own research to itself via Claude's `link_research`
  creates a harmless but invisible duplicate association; the panes hide it.
  A guard is planned.
