# MaughamPhone 0.1.2

_Released 2026-06-03_

A reader-fidelity release: screenplays and title pages now render the way they do
on the Mac, and annotation styling matches across your devices.

## What's new
- **Screenplay emphasis now renders.** Bold, italic, and underline inside action
  and dialogue (`*italic*`, `**bold**`, `_underline_`) now show in the reader —
  previously the markers were ignored and the text rendered plain.
- **Title pages are properly styled.** Each field is now laid out by role — the
  title large and bold, credit and source in italics, the rest sized and aligned to
  match the Mac — instead of one flat block.
- **Section headings are underlined**, matching the Mac editor.
- **Consistent annotation styling.** Annotation icons and labels (comment,
  suggested change, query, craft note) now match the Mac exactly.

## Notes
- Reading and capture are otherwise unchanged from 0.1.0/0.1.1; this release is
  about matching the Mac's rendering and styling.
- Under the hood, the phone and Mac now share single definitions of the things they
  both touch (screenplay styling decisions, file naming, the project manifest
  format), so the two apps stay in lockstep.
- Distributed via TestFlight.

## Known issues
- No live cross-device refresh yet — lists update on appear, pull-to-refresh, or
  after a phone-side action.
- Voice transcription refinement happens on the Mac (Apple Silicon); the phone keeps
  its on-device draft if the Mac can't improve it.
