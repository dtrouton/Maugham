# MaughamPhone 0.16.0

_Released 2026-11-01_

Tables and figures on your iPhone. Pairs with Maugham 0.43.0 — **update your
Mac and your iPhone together.**

## What's new

- **Tables show as tables.** In the Read tab, a figure line draws its table —
  header, rows, the ruled total, and "+ N more rows" — computed from the
  book, with numbers in the book's language. Wide tables scroll sideways
  inside the card. Pictures show as pictures.
- **No more raw labels.** Headings and lines no longer show `{#…}` markers.
- **Notes on cells.** A cell with an open note shows a dot. The Annotations
  tab lists notes on tables with the cell they point at and, for a
  suggestion, the value it suggests. Reject and Archive work here; Accept a
  table suggestion on your Mac.

## Known issues

- A table that hasn't downloaded yet says so; tap Retry once it has.
- The phone card shows no "Table 3" number and no "Research has changed"
  banner; your Mac shows both.
- Cards load when you open a chapter, not as changes arrive from your Mac.
