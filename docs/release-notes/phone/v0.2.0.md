# MaughamPhone 0.2.0

_Released 2026-07-02_

Triage Claude's notes by where they live. The Annotations tab is now a
**project → chapter → notes** drill-down instead of one long cross-project list,
with an **Open / All** switch for reviewing what you've already handled.

## New

- **Drill into a project, then a chapter.** The Annotations tab opens on your
  projects (most-recent first). Tap a project to see its chapters/pieces that have
  open notes — listed in binder order, grouped under their part/act headers, each
  with a count — then tap a chapter to read just its notes. A project with notes in
  only one document skips straight to them.
- **Open / All.** A switch at the top of the Annotations tab. **Open** (the default)
  is your triage list — only notes still needing a decision. Flip to **All** to bring
  back chapters and projects you've fully handled, so you can review what you
  accepted, rejected, or archived; resolved notes appear in a dimmed "Resolved"
  section with a status label.
- **Review resolved notes read-only.** Opening a resolved note shows its recorded
  outcome (Accepted / Rejected / Archived and any note you left) — no action buttons,
  and it no longer mislabels a note you resolved as "resolved on another device."

## Notes
- Counts stay in sync as you triage: accepting/rejecting/archiving a note updates the
  chapter and project counts, and a fully-handled chapter drops out of the Open list.
- Nothing about how notes are stored changed — this is a phone-side reading and
  triage improvement over the same op log the Mac writes. No Mac update required.
- Distributed via TestFlight.

## Known issues
- No live cross-device refresh yet — lists update on appear, pull-to-refresh, or
  after a phone-side action.
- Re-opening a resolved note (undoing a decision) from the phone isn't available yet;
  resolved review is read-only for now.
