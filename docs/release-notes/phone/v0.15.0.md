# MaughamPhone 0.15.0

_Released YYYY-MM-DD_

Your iPhone joins your books as you. Pairs with Maugham 0.42.0 — **update your
Mac and your iPhone together.**

## What's new

- **Your Mac recognises your iPhone.** With iCloud Keychain on and the same
  Apple ID on both, the phone picks up the key your Mac made, and the first
  time it writes in a book — a capture into the Inbox, or something done to a
  note — it says whose it is. The Mac that holds the book then lets it in as
  you, with no panel.
- **Your iPhone gets a proper name.** Where iOS calls it just "iPhone", Maugham
  calls it *Denver's iPhone*, using the first name on your key. A name you gave
  the phone yourself is never replaced.
- **Settings → This device says whether your other devices recognise this
  phone**, and if not, why: the key hasn't arrived from your Mac yet, iCloud
  Keychain isn't reachable, or the phone has no key of its own to sign with.
  It updates when you pull to refresh or come back to the app.

## Known issues

- The phone never makes the key itself. Until it arrives from one of your
  Macs, the Mac asks who the phone is, once; after that the phone goes into
  your other books silently.
- A phone that has only read a book hasn't said whose it is there yet. It
  does the first time it writes in that book.
