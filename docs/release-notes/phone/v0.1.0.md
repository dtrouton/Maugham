# MaughamPhone 0.1.0

_Released 2026-05-31_

The first release of the MaughamPhone companion — Maugham for iPhone, for capturing
ideas away from your desk and reading + triaging on the go. It works against the
same iCloud-Drive projects folder as the Mac app.

## What's new

- **Capture** — jot a quick text note, snap a photo reference, or record a voice memo
  straight into your project's inbox. Voice notes get an instant on-device draft; the
  Mac refines the transcript when it next sees the capture.
- **Read** — browse your projects and open any manuscript. Prose renders as clean
  Markdown; screenplays render with semantic Fountain styling (title page, scene
  headings, character cues, dialogue, transitions).
- **Annotations** — review Claude's open notes across every project and Accept /
  Reject / Archive them, with the paragraph context shown. Optional Face ID gate.
- **Settings** — pick your iCloud-Drive projects folder, check capture permissions,
  toggle the Face ID gate.

## Notes

- Reads and writes the same security-scoped iCloud-Drive folder as the Mac app; there
  is no separate account or sign-in.
- The phone never edits your manuscript text — captures land in the inbox and
  annotation actions append to the op log, exactly like the Mac.
- Distributed via TestFlight (internal testing).

## Known issues

- No live cross-device refresh yet — lists update on appear, pull-to-refresh, or
  after a phone-side action.
- Voice transcription refinement happens on the Mac (Apple Silicon); the phone keeps
  its on-device draft if the Mac can't improve it.
