# MaughamPhone 0.1.3

_Released 2026-06-07_

A reader-fidelity patch: nested and combined emphasis now render correctly.

## Fixes
- **Nested and combined emphasis renders correctly.** Text that's both bold and
  italic (`***like this***`), or emphasis nested inside emphasis, now displays
  properly in the reader — previously overlapping styles could clobber each other
  and the text rendered plain.

## Notes
- Otherwise unchanged from 0.1.2; this is a rendering fix plus internal hardening
  shared with the Mac (the phone and Mac continue to share single definitions of
  the things they both touch).
- Distributed via TestFlight.

## Known issues
- No live cross-device refresh yet — lists update on appear, pull-to-refresh, or
  after a phone-side action.
- Voice transcription refinement happens on the Mac (Apple Silicon); the phone keeps
  its on-device draft if the Mac can't improve it.
