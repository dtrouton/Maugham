# MaughamPhone 0.13.0

_Released 2026-XX-XX_ <!-- draft: Denver names the number and the date -->

The pairing release for Mac 0.39.0, people and admission. The phone can now
tell you where it stands in each book, and the Mac can let it in.

## What's new

- **Settings has a This device section**, first in the list. Your phone's
  four-character **code** on its own line — the one thing to compare when
  the Mac asks who this machine is — and then one line for each book this
  phone has been in: *Denver on Denver's MacBook's chain since 9 Sep*, or
  *Not yet admitted — the Mac will ask*.
- **Pull down to refresh it.** The answer changes on the Mac, so the phone
  re-reads on appear and on a pull rather than guessing.
- **Nothing to press.** Admitting is a Mac act in this release. The phone
  says where it stands, in the same words the Mac's own People & Devices
  list uses about itself, so the two can't tell you different things.
- **A retired phone is told so**, and told what it means: it goes on
  writing into its own copy while every other machine holds that work back.
- **Declaring itself costs less.** The phone used to read and verify the
  whole registry folder before every capture and every annotation, just to
  decide it had nothing to add. It now remembers what it declared, per
  book, and a second write into the same book touches the disk not at all.

## Known issues

- **Until your Mac admits this phone, its annotations and captures are
  held** — they will not appear on the Mac. Nothing is lost: open the
  project on the Mac, answer the panel, and all of it arrives at once.
- The phone reads the registry and never writes into it. It holds no root
  and cannot admit itself, which is deliberate.
