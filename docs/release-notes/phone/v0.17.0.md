# MaughamPhone 0.17.0

_Released 2026-11-16_

Suggestions on your iPhone follow the same rule as on your Mac. Pairs with
Maugham 0.47.0 — **update your Mac and your iPhone together.**

## Fixes

- **A suggestion is never applied twice.** Accepting a suggestion whose change
  is already in the text marks it Accepted and leaves the text alone. Before,
  a suggestion reopened over text that already held it could have its change
  applied a second time.
- **Reopen & Revert works on a suggestion that changed nothing.** If your Mac
  accepted a suggestion without touching the text, Reopen & Revert on the
  phone now reopens it and leaves your words as they are. Before, it showed an
  error.
- **Table cards follow who is trusted.** When the people allowed to write in a
  book change, the tables in the Read tab are worked out again instead of
  showing what was loaded before.

## Known issues

- A table that hasn't downloaded yet says so; tap Retry once it has.
- The phone card shows no "Table 3" number and no "Research has changed"
  banner; your Mac shows both.
- Cards load when you open a chapter, not as changes arrive from your Mac.
