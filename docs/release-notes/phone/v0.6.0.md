# MaughamPhone 0.6.0

_Released 2026-07-11_

The sensory palette comes to the phone — because the tram-rattle and the
pre-storm light happen out here, not at your desk.

## What's new

- **Aim a capture at a palette subject.** The Capture tab gains an Aim
  row: pick one of the project's palette cards (or type a new subject),
  optionally tag the sense — sight, sound, smell, touch, taste — and every
  photo, voice note, or text capture you take carries the aim until you
  clear it or switch projects. Aiming is never required; plain inbox
  capture is untouched. On the Mac, aimed entries promote onto their card
  in one click.
- **Browse the palette in Read.** A project's Read view gains a Palette
  section: each card shows its images, colour swatches, sense-grouped
  notes, and freeform text — reference in your pocket while you're away
  from the manuscript. The craft-intent doc is there too. Read-only by
  design: cards are edited on the Mac.
- Palette cards no longer clutter the Research list in Read — they live in
  their own section now.

## Compatibility

- Pairs with **Maugham 0.20.0** on the Mac (which adds promote-into-card
  for aimed captures). Both directions are tolerant: older Macs simply
  see aimed captures as ordinary inbox entries.
