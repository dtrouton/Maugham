# Maugham 0.5.1

_Released 2026-06-01_

A focused round of editor fixes — typing at the bottom of a long document, focus
mode, and typewriter scrolling all behave the way they should now.

## Fixes
- **No more scroll jump when typing at the bottom of a long document.** Pressing
  space or delete near the end of a long manuscript no longer flicks the view to
  the top and back. The editor now holds your scroll position steady through each
  keystroke.
- **Paragraph focus highlights the right paragraph.** With paragraph focus on,
  typing at the *end* of a paragraph kept the dimming on the *following*
  paragraph. The active paragraph now stays lit while you write at its end.
- **Typewriter scrolling stays centered.** The active line now holds at the
  vertical center of the window even while you write at the very end of a
  document — previously it pinned to the bottom edge unless there was already
  text below the cursor. (This reserves some blank space above the first line and
  below the last line; that headroom is what lets the line stay centered.)
