# Maugham 0.12.6

_Released 2026-07-02_

A reliability release: a deep audit of the record-keeping system behind your
manuscripts, with fixes for two rare but serious bugs, stronger safety nets,
and faster big projects.

## Fixes

- **Deleting a paragraph now always sticks.** If deleting (or reordering) a
  paragraph was the very last thing you did before quitting, the deletion
  could quietly come back on your next launch. Fixed — ordering-only changes
  are now recorded just like typing.
- **A project can no longer open empty when its internal records are missing.**
  If Maugham's internal records for a document were lost (a backup tool that
  skipped hidden folders, or an interrupted first import) while the manuscript
  file itself was fine, the document could open blank. Now Maugham rebuilds its
  records from the file and your writing opens intact.
- **Edits made outside Maugham while it was closed are kept as safety copies.**
  Maugham remains the source of truth for manuscripts, but if a file was
  changed by another app while Maugham wasn't running, the outside version is
  now preserved under the project's `.maugham/conflicts/` folder before Maugham
  restores its own — same protection you already had for edits made while the
  app was open. Safety copies are also filed in the right place for Collection
  pieces now, and tidied automatically so they can't pile up forever.

## Faster and fresher

- **Big projects open and search faster.** Word counts now fill in quietly in
  the background instead of holding up the window, and searching no longer
  re-reads every document's full history on each keystroke.
- **Compile, search, and Claude's link tools always see your newest words.**
  Previously the last minute or so of typing in an open document could be
  missing from a compiled PDF/EPUB or a search result; everything now reads the
  live document.
- **Switching between pieces in a Collection is instant again**, even after a
  very large screenplay has been open in the same session.

## Notes

- Cross-device syncing got quieter and more careful in unusual situations
  (files arriving from iCloud out of order, another device's records syncing
  late). Nothing changes in day-to-day use.
- The iPhone companion app is unchanged (it ships separately).
