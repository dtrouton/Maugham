# Maugham 0.31.0

_Released 2026-08-21_

The book gets a publishing department. Translation and design stop being
things you drive from outside with tools and become people you work with
inside the app — a named translator per language and a book designer,
each briefed on your intent, your bible, your rulings, and each answerable
to you at a desk built for it.

## What you'll notice

- **Publish opens on the department desk** (⌘⌥K). A Design row and one
  row per language edition: who's translating, how much of the book is
  fresh, stale, or missing in that language, and how many of their
  questions you still owe an answer.
- **Your translators have names.** Spanish reads under **Cortázar**,
  French under **Baudelaire**, German under **Tieck**, Japanese under
  **Motoyuki** — rename anyone from their row. A language outside the
  presets asks you to name its translator before the first run. **Add
  Language…** starts a brand-new edition right on the desk.
- **Run a translation on the open chapter.** One click on the language's
  row translates whatever is stale or missing there, briefed on your
  craft intent, the bible, your review rulings, and that edition's own
  brief. Questions land in your queue signed by the translator; nothing
  is written unless the round finishes cleanly — and if you edit a
  paragraph while the round is thinking, that round is refused by name
  rather than silently translating text you've already moved past.
- **Each edition has a brief.** `editions/es.md` is yours the way the
  craft intent is: register, idiom policy, who characters are when the
  language forces a choice English didn't make. It carries rulings, and
  **Answer as ruling…** on a translator's question writes your answer
  into the brief and onto the thread in one act — doctrine every future
  round is briefed on, not a buried reply.
- **Tschichold designs the book.** Give the Design row a direction —
  "square album, warmer paper, looser leading" — and the designer
  proposes a spec plus complete templates, demonstrated as **sample
  pages** compiled against a staged copy. Nothing touches your live
  templates until you approve.
- **The gate has four verbs.** Sample pages open in the centre with the
  spec beside them: **Approve** promotes the staged set (your originals
  backed up first), **Request Changes** iterates on the same warm
  session, **Revert** takes an approved design back whole, **Finalize**
  accepts it permanently — the one irreversible step, and it asks first.
  Every refusal explains itself in words.
- The manuscript stays untouchable from outside: the spawned sessions
  read and return; Maugham writes at ingest, through the same paths the
  MCP tools use. Claude Desktop's `write_translation` and template tools
  keep working as the manual override.

## Under the hood

- Manifest schema 7 → 8 (`productionRoles`, the edition-brief statement
  kind). **This pairs with MaughamPhone 0.10.0** — older phone builds
  refuse a schema-8 project rather than mis-read it.
- New MCP tool `read_edition_brief` (56 tools); `translation_status` now
  names each language's translator; the confined compiler/translator/
  designer sessions share one allowlist, one shutdown contract, and one
  translation write path with the outside tools.
