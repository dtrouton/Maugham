# Maugham 0.19.0

_Released 2026-07-10_

A release about getting the sensation of things right. Declare what a story
needs, gather the sights and sounds and smells it should carry, and then ask
Claude to audit whether the prose delivers — against *your* standard, not a
generic one. Not every story wants thick texture; deciding that is the point.

## What's new

- **Craft intent.** An optional, freeform note about what this piece needs —
  "the port scenes should smell; keep the narrator spare." Add one from the
  inspector (**Add craft intent…**); each piece in a collection can carry its
  own. Not writing one is a valid choice, and nothing will ever nag you about
  it.
- **The Palette.** A new binder segment for sensory reference: cards for
  places, characters, and motifs, laid out as a visual wall. Each card holds
  images, colour swatches, and sense-tagged notes (sight / sound / smell /
  touch / taste), plus room for freeform thoughts.
- **A visual card editor — no markdown required.** Drag images in from Finder
  *or straight from your browser*, or paste them. Pick swatches with the
  colour picker, or use the **eyedropper** to sample a colour right off one
  of your reference images. Tag notes by sense with one click. (On disk a
  card is still clean, portable markdown — Maugham writes it for you.)
- **Write against a card.** ⌘⌥7 opens any palette card read-only beside the
  editor — the flat's swatches and tram-rattle in view while you draft the
  scene set there. Cards with swatches but no image show their colours on
  the wall.
- **The sense pass.** Claude can now read your craft intent and palette over
  MCP (three new read-only tools: `read_craft_intent`, `list_palette_cards`,
  `read_palette_card` — images included) and audit a draft against them,
  leaving paragraph-anchored notes you triage in the Annotations pane like
  any other. The full prompt recipe ships in Help (⌘?) under **The Sense
  Pass**. If you haven't declared an intent, Claude is told to ask rather
  than invent a standard for you.

## Fixes

- Dragging an image from a browser now works in every drop zone — palette
  cards and the Research pane alike. (Real files keep their names; bare
  links are never downloaded.)
- An explicit **Paste** button on the card's image well, so pasting an image
  no longer depends on keyboard focus.

## Known issues

- Renaming the palette's "Palette" folder through the Research pane detaches
  the wall from your cards (they remain safe as research notes). Leave the
  folder name alone for now; a sturdier identity is planned.
- A browser-dropped image imported into Research (not a palette card) gets a
  timestamp for a title; rename it as you like.
