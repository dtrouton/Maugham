# Maugham 0.6.1

_Released 2026-06-03_

A consistency release: the Mac and the MaughamPhone companion now agree, by
construction, on how shared things look and are stored — and a couple of small
display fixes come along for the ride.

## Fixes
- **Annotation cards no longer show editing markup.** When Claude annotated a
  paragraph that contained inline task markers, the annotation's before/after card
  could show that raw markup as noise. It's now stripped for display, matching the
  phone.

## What's new
- **Consistent annotation styling across devices.** Annotation kinds (comment,
  suggested change, query, craft note) now use the same icon and label on the Mac
  and on your iPhone — they're defined once and shared, so they can't drift apart.

## Notes
- Under the hood this release establishes a single shared source of truth for the
  things the Mac app and the iPhone companion both touch — file naming, the project
  manifest format, screenplay styling decisions — so the two apps stay in lockstep
  as both evolve. No change to your projects or files; everything on disk is
  unchanged.
- Signing, notarization, and in-place updates from 0.6.0 are unchanged.
