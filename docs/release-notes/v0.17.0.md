# Maugham 0.17.0

_Released 2026-07-09_

Accepting one of Claude's suggestions is no longer a one-way door. ⌘Z takes
it back, a new Revert button works on any accepted suggestion at any time,
and History rewinds put suggestions back the way the timeline says they
should be. A crash and a text-mangling bug in the same neighborhood are
fixed.

## What's new

- **⌘Z undoes an accepted suggestion.** Accept a suggestion, change your
  mind, press ⌘Z: the paragraph returns to what it was and the suggestion
  reopens in the Annotations pane. ⇧⌘Z re-applies it, keeping any reply you
  attached. ⌘Z reaches the most recent accept; for anything older, use
  Revert (below).
- **Revert any accepted suggestion.** Accepted suggestions (visible with
  resolved annotations shown) now have a **Revert** button — days later, in
  any order. If you've edited the paragraph since accepting, Maugham asks
  before touching it. A revert is itself undoable with ⌘Z.
- **Rewind and suggestions now agree.** Rewinding History to before an
  accept used to leave the suggestion marked "accepted" while its change
  had vanished — it looked lost. Now the rewind reopens it (or archives it,
  if the rewind removed the paragraph it pointed at), and the rewind preview
  tells you how many suggestions will reopen. Reverts also appear as their
  own entries in the History pane.

## Fixes

- **Fixed a crash pressing ⌘Z after annotation activity.** Accepting a
  suggestion (and other changes arriving from outside the editor) could
  leave the undo history pointing at text that no longer existed; the next
  ⌘Z crashed the app. All such paths now keep the undo history sound —
  including switching documents.
- **Suggestions anchored to a phrase no longer paste a whole paragraph into
  it.** When Claude proposed a full-paragraph rewrite but anchored it to a
  quoted phrase, accepting spliced the entire paragraph into the middle of
  itself. Maugham now recognizes the mismatch and applies the rewrite
  cleanly — and the suggestion tool's contract has been rewritten so Claude
  authors them correctly in the first place.

## Compatibility

- **Update the Mac app and MaughamPhone together** (this release pairs with
  MaughamPhone 0.4.0). Projects *created* by Maugham 0.17.0 use a newer
  project format that older app versions refuse to open. Existing projects
  are unaffected.

## Known issues

- ⌘Z after an accept restores your undo point for that accept but not for
  typing done just before it — the Revert button and History cover anything
  ⌘Z can't reach.
