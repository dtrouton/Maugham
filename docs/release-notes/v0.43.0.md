# Maugham 0.43.0

_Released 2026-11-01_

Reports. A new kind of project for business and non-fiction writing — tables,
figures, footnotes, citations and cross-references, published to PDF, EPUB and
Word — with real tables you edit like a spreadsheet. Pairs with MaughamPhone
0.16.0 — **update your Mac and your iPhone together.** Books saved by this
version can't be opened by older versions.

## What's new

- **Report projects.** New project ▸ Report sets up a book for business
  writing: numbered headings, a list of figures and tables, references in
  author–date style, and a publishing check that refuses to publish a report
  with broken references or error cells. Other kinds of project can use the
  same syntax; their problems warn instead of refusing.
- **Footnotes, citations and cross-references** in plain text. Insert ▸
  Footnote (⌘⇧N), a citation picker (⌘⇧C) over Research items with citation
  details, and a cross-reference picker (⌘⇧X) for figures, tables and
  sections. Labels show faintly beneath their markers; broken ones are
  underlined.
- **Tables are figures you edit in place.** Insert ▸ Table (⌘⇧T), or Convert
  to Table Figure on a hand-typed table. The table draws as a card under its
  line; double-click (or Return on the caption) opens a grid in the text with
  a formula bar, number formats, a header and total row, sorting, fill, copy
  and paste from Excel, Sheets and Numbers, and ⤢ to open it in its own
  window. One ⌘Z after folding undoes the whole visit.
- **Formulas that match Excel**, including SUM, AVERAGE, ROUND, IF, lookups,
  dates, NPV, IRR, XNPV, XIRR and the annuity functions. References follow
  inserted and deleted rows, and can read other tables and Research.
- **The Figures area.** Every table and picture in the book in one place, with
  its number, where it's used, and an inspector. Drag a figure into the text
  or into Research; Use in Document makes a figure from a Research table,
  picture or PDF; Send to Research parks it and brings it back later.
- **Research tables.** A CSV or TSV in Research opens in the same grid. Every
  save keeps the previous version — the inspector lists them, marks the ones
  Claude replaced, and offers Restore previous version.
- **Publishing.** PDF, EPUB and Word print tables from the computed values,
  with numbers and dates in each edition's language, a repeated header and a
  ruled total row. Word exports are drafts you can send for review.
- **Claude and your figures.** Claude can list and read your figures and
  Research tables, create and write Research tables, and leave notes and
  suggestions on a table, a cell or a range. A noted cell shows a dot; select
  it in the grid to see the note in a strip with Accept, Got it and Reject.
  Accepting writes the cell as you. Clicking a figure note in the review
  queue opens the table at that cell.
- **Translated table labels.** A translated edition prints its own row and
  column labels; numbers and formulas stay shared. The translator now covers
  the tables a chapter uses, and the translation check counts their labels.

## Fixes

- Spanish and Serbian editions now print their own number separators on a
  Mac set to another region.
- Many fixes from two codebase audits: undo at the edges of runs, canvas
  keyboard flow, publishing and MCP hardening, the updater, section headers,
  and right-column layout.

## Known issues

- Translation status now counts a chapter with no translation as missing once
  its language has translations elsewhere in the edition, matching what the
  publishing check already refuses.
- The suggestion strip in the grid can't yet be reached from the keyboard.
- Research notes don't keep versions yet; only Research tables do.
