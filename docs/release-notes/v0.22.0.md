# Maugham 0.22.0

_Released 2026-07-17_

This one is about tidying your research. Collections have always let
research live at the collection level or inside a piece — but moving
something from one home to the other meant dragging files around in
Finder, which Maugham then studiously ignored. Now it's a drag, a
right-click, or a word to Claude.

## What you'll notice

- **Move research between shared and piece.** Drag a note, image, link,
  or a whole folder from the Shared section into a piece's section (or
  back, or straight onto an empty section) and it moves — files, image
  attachments, and all. A **Move to ▸** menu on the right-click does the
  same without the aim.
- **Folders in collections finally work.** Research groups in a
  collection render as real disclosure trees, accept drops, and offer
  New Note / New Group / Add File / Add Link inside — they were
  decorative before. Groups work inside pieces, too.
- **Select more than one thing.** ⌘-click and ⇧-click select multiple
  research items; drag one and the whole selection comes along. Move or
  delete the lot in one gesture.
- **The right pane understands piece folders.** A folder of research
  inside a piece shows its *contents* in the Research pane, not an inert
  folder row.
- **Claude can re-file research.** A new `move_research_item` tool lets
  Claude move batches of research between shared, groups, and pieces
  during a reorganisation conversation — 48 tools total.

## Link behaviour, refined

Moving research into a piece makes it that piece's research; moving it
out ends that association — no ghost links left behind. But links you
added *by hand* are never destroyed: they lie dormant while the item
lives in the piece and resurface in Linked Research when it moves out.

## Under the hood

- Cross-group moves now carry a note's image-attachments folder (a
  long-standing quiet bug — embedded images broke when a note changed
  folders).
- Deleting a moved web-link no longer risks a crash on its file-less
  path.
- Error messages from failed moves now say what actually went wrong
  instead of "error 6".
- The Palette group refuses moves that would break the palette wall,
  with a readable explanation.

## Compatibility

- No manuscript, op-log, or schema changes. Phone pairing unchanged —
  MaughamPhone 0.7.0 continues to work; no phone update required.
