# Maugham 0.49.1

_Released 2026-11-26_

_Mac only: the phone app is unchanged._

## What's new

- **To resolve's head folds, and the list gets the room.** The passes, the latest letter, the round's and the check's settings and both Ask about… fields used to fill most of the column above what's waiting on you. Now the head is folded until you open it, three short rows at most:
  - where the chapter stands (*Revising · Line in progress*, *Final · all four passes done*);
  - **Run Round**, and **Check Writing** on a chapter never checked or **Reread** once it has been, with **Cancel** while something runs;
  - one line, only when there's something to say: a run in progress (always shown while it runs), a failure, a check that found nothing new, the latest letter (*Letter from Lish · 2 hours ago*; click it to open the head), the pass-order nudge, or what you've asked about.

  The chevron at the end of the first row opens and folds the head, and the window remembers your choice. When the window is short, the open head scrolls within itself so the list never disappears. A translated edition's head and a chapter read in a language fold the same way. Every key works as before, and File ▸ Translate… is answered with the head folded.
- **No name you already know.** Over the chapter you have open, To resolve no longer repeats its name above the list or in the head: you have it selected. A chapter that isn't the one in the middle, a Part and the Book are still named. VoiceOver still hears the chapter's name, as To resolve's heading.
- **Short lists drop their group headers.** Under five items, each item carries a small caption saying where it sits instead, so a list of three things takes three rows, not six. The note that a list may still be loading rides on the first line rather than taking a row of its own.
- **A quieter row above the list.** Select Notes and New To-Do stay plain buttons beside the chips; only in a column too narrow for them do they fold into one **…** menu. While you select notes, **Done** sits on that row. Over an empty list there's no row at all: New To-Do's **+** sits at the end of *Nothing to resolve*.

## Fixes

- **New To-Do's Cancel button is no longer cut to "Ca…".** The scope choice and the buttons now sit on separate rows.
