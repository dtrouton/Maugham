# Maugham 0.36.0

_Released 2026-09-06_

Two loops, two readers. Author's ⌘R is now a **check** and Review's Run
Round is a **round**, and the two never share a reader, a lane, a round
number, a delta rule or an output shape. Le Guin, the coach, is Author's
reader full stop — she keeps reading a piece after it enters a pass, and
she never files a round. Beside her, Author gains a reader of your own: **a
first reader**, one specific person you name and describe, who reports what
happened in her as she read and never proposes a fix. A round reads the
piece whole, needs an editor, and is Review's alone. Three plans, one
milestone, shipped whole. (ADR 0031.)

## What's new

- **Two verbs, one substrate.** ⌘R in Author is a check: the delta since
  your last check, read by the coach or your first reader, filed in no
  lane, numbered nowhere, its notes unstamped and signed by the reader.
  Run Round in Review is a round: the whole piece, every time, read by the
  piece's stage editor, numbered in that pass's lane, its notes
  pass-stamped. The File menu says which loop you are standing in —
  **Check Writing / Reread** in Author, **Run Round / Fresh Eyes** in
  Review — and the two keys are unchanged.
- **The coach leaves the board.** Le Guin is a seat in Project Settings
  and nothing on the review board: no seat row, no lane in the cockpit.
  A round needs a pass — with none set, the cockpit reads *Set a pass to
  run a round* and ⌘R in Review flashes the same sentence and starts
  nothing.
- **Your own first reader.** Name her in Project Settings (**First
  reader**, beneath the coach's row) and press **Describe…** to open her
  statement (⌘⌥Y): who she is, what she reads, what she loves, what she
  will not sit through. Her `## Rulings` are standing instructions —
  answering one of her notes with **Answer as ruling…** in Review's queue
  files there. She reports four things — where the dream broke, what she
  now believes, where she got bored, where she got lost — quoting the
  words that did it, never craft vocabulary, never a change; her letter is
  the short reader form (the answer to your ask, what the piece seems to
  be about, what she loved and why, at most one question), on a Reread as
  much as on a check. She is briefed on neither your lessons ledger nor
  your process, and the books pinned to the piece reach her as her taste.
- **The reader picker.** The Diagnostics header's reader line is a menu:
  Le Guin, your first reader, or Claude — only the readers this project
  actually has, with **Define a first reader…** when none is named. The
  choice is remembered per project on this Mac.
- **Reread.** ⌘⇧R in Author, and a button in the Diagnostics header: the
  same reader over the whole piece, cold, still a check. Fresh Eyes is
  Review's cold round.
- **One ask per loop.** The ask you type in Author is for the reader who
  checks; the one in Review is for the editor who runs the round.
- **The record remembers who read.** A check's standing letter, Keep this
  letter and Add to intent sign with the reader who actually wrote it,
  even after you change readers or vacate the seat.
- **`read_first_reader`** joins the MCP spine (60 tools): Claude reads
  her name, description and instructions, and never writes them.

## Fixes

- A legacy check written under the coach's old `workshop` lane now loads
  as the check it was — Author's pane no longer goes blank over a piece
  you checked last week, and Review no longer shows the coach's letter as
  a standing round.
- The unread badge on Diagnostics is Author's: a round no longer clears
  it, and a check no longer clears a round's count.
- Running the test gate while the dev app is open no longer deletes the
  app's MCP socket (every check afterwards failed with *couldn't be read
  as notes* until a relaunch); the compiler's per-session MCP config
  files no longer leak into the temp directory.
- A failed statement action shows its message rather than a raw error
  dump.

## Known issues

- A round's conformance strains are recorded but drawn nowhere in Review
  yet; Author's Diagnostics pane draws the check's.
- One warm Claude session serves both loops in a window; if a round after
  a long check session reads tired, that is the reason.
