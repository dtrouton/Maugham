## 2. Synthesis over the maps (the sweep payoff)

**Collision 1: the newly awaited quit × the close machinery.**
- The tracer's surprise was that 493275f brings the whole `Document.close` back into the quit, seal and segment rotation included. Before, the willTerminate close was never awaited.
- Its thread-split map shows the clock is a main-actor Task while `SegmentSealer.sealTail` is synchronous on main.
- Together: a "bounded" wait that a stalled coordinated read can hang (Q4), and an ordering that spends the deadline on maintenance before data (Q5).
- Class: **two correct features interacting.**

**Collision 2: a snapshot before an await, a clear after it (H1).**
- The tracer's "invariants assumed" entry ("nothing records into `pending` between the snapshot and the clear") was guaranteed nowhere.
- The OpLogStore → `JSONLAppendStore` hop to the generic executor (Swift 5.10) makes the window real.
- The ordering-flag reset turns a lost keystroke, which heals at the next edit, into a lost paragraph, which does not heal.
- This is also the same shape as #150's new-note row loss: `moveResearchItems` re-inserts a pre-await snapshot.
- **One pattern, two places:** state captured before an await and written back wholesale after it.

**Collision 3: two versions of one rule, for the fourth week running.**
- Hunter 2's duplication map: manuscript `RewindImpact.travelStatusChanges` against Core `FigureRestoreNotePlan`. The figure walk got R-B / F2; the manuscript walk did not (S2).
- Hunter 2's table-vs-note door comparison: only the note door knows the move span (T4).
- Hunter 1: "file gone vs in flight" is answered in two places and missing from a third (R3).
- Hunter 1: compare-and-swap was added to Replace but not to the four sibling read-modify-write callers (R5).
- The 11-15 note asked plan briefs to grep for an existing spelling of a rule. These four show the next step: **when a fix changes one spelling, it must change its twins.**
