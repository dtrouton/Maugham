## Part 1 — the four that must not be skimmed

**1.1 Drag on empty canvas.** An outline follows your pointer as you sweep, and
on release you get a labelled region.
*Failure:* the outline was the last thing added to this slice, because as
originally planned you swept an area and saw nothing until you let go. If it's
missing you cannot tell a drag that is drawing from a drag doing nothing.

**1.2 Sweep a region straight around two existing cards, with a third only
clipped at its edge.** The two whose **middles** are inside join it — check the
inspector's "Lives here" — and the clipped one does not. Then press ⌘Z **once**:
the region goes and both cards are loose again.
*Failure:* this check was exactly backwards until 2026-07-28, when Denver ruled
that **creation absorbs** (spec §4.2 amendment). If the clipped card joined,
the centre rule is not being used. If one ⌘Z leaves the region gone but the
membership behind — or takes two presses — the absorption was registered outside
the create gesture.

**1.2b Now drag that region by its label bar clean over a card it does NOT own,
then resize it past another one.** Neither is absorbed.
*Failure:* geometry has reached membership through a **transition**, which is
the half the firewall still holds. Every comparable tool ships a version of this
bug — tldraw's survives *despite* storing membership explicitly — and this is
now the interesting half of the check, since creation deliberately does absorb.

**1.2c Sweep a region across a *collapsed* region's frame.** You have to start
outside every region — a sweep refuses to begin inside one. Its hidden residents
are not absorbed and nothing appears to vanish.
*Failure:* absorbing something the writer cannot see is the one case where "you
pointed at it" isn't true, which is why hidden cards are excluded.

**1.2d ⊘ Double-click *inside* a selected region, type a word, and do not click
away.** The new card appears under "Lives here" immediately.
*Failure:* the join happens after the only structural bump on that path, so the
inspector currently sees it by a beat of luck rather than by guarantee. There is
now a bump inside the join to make it a guarantee — but **no test can see the
difference**, because anything reading the rows after the gesture settles
recomputes and sees the join either way. This check is the only observer.

**1.3 Drag a card so its middle lands inside the region, and release.** It joins.
Now **look at the inspector without clicking anything else** — the card is listed
under "Lives here", and it is *gone* from the "Cite a Card" menu.
*Failure:* this is the whole-branch review's finding. Two structural counters
drifted apart, so the inspector kept showing a region as empty while the canvas
drew it correctly. **Do not click away first** — clicking is what used to mask it.

**1.4 Drag the region by its label bar.** The card you dropped in travels with
it. Any card you did *not* drop in stays exactly where it is.
*Failure:* a visitor is being treated as luggage, or residents aren't being
carried at all.

## Part 2 — undo, which broke three separate ways during the build

**2.1 ⌘Z once after the region drag.** The region *and* every card it carried go
back together, in one press.

**2.2 Drop a card into a region, then ⌘Z once.** The card returns to where it
started **and is no longer a member** — one gesture, one undo.

**2.2b ⊘ Sweep a region that absorbs several cards, ⌘Z, then ⇧⌘Z.** Both halves
return in one press — the region *and* every membership it took.
*Failure:* redo is sound by construction (undo is snapshot-based, so the
memberships cannot be a separate step) but nothing pins it for a sweep
specifically.

**2.3 Select a card, press ⌫, then ⌘Z.** The card comes back **with its words**.
Check the text is really there, not just the card.
*Failure:* the words live in `canvas.md` and nowhere else. This is the one
Critical the slice found.

**2.4 Press and hold the mouse on a card, and while still holding, press ⌫.**
Nothing is deleted and you get a beep.
*Failure:* a delete arriving mid-gesture used to vanish — the card went and no
undo step was recorded at all.

**2.5 Now the awkward one.** Double-click a **region's own label bar** (this
mints a scrap on top of it — see 7.1). Type a sentence. Click back on the label
bar. Then check the Edit menu: it reads **"Undo Edit Scrap"**, not "Undo Rename
Region" or anything else.
*Failure:* an edit made from one column nesting inside a bracket opened by the
other. It registers nothing and rides into your next step, so a ⌘Z aimed at a
sentence takes something else with it.

**2.6 Press ⌫ with nothing selected.** A beep, and nothing happens.
*Failure:* a key that does nothing *and* swallows the beep is indistinguishable
from a broken app.

## Part 3 — the inspector

**3.1 Select a region and rename it.** One ⌘Z takes the rename back, once.
*Failure:* one undo step per keystroke.

**3.2 Collapse it.** Its resident cards vanish from the canvas, the label says how
many are hidden, and clicking where one was does nothing.

**3.3 Expand it again.** They come back, clickable.

**3.4 Bind it to a piece**, then quit and reopen. The binding survived.
*(Nothing consumes it yet — that's a later milestone. The inspector is how you
can see it at all.)*

**3.5 Use "Cite a Card" to cite a card that lives elsewhere.** It appears in the
region as a small chip with a hairline running to the real card. **The real card
does not move.**
*Failure:* a reference that renders like the thing itself. You must be able to
tell which one is real.

**3.6 Remove it from the "Appears here" list.** The chip goes; the card doesn't.

## Part 4 — the accepted cost, made visible

**4.1 Drag a resident far outside its region.** A tether draws between them, and
it grows *while you drag*, not on release.
*Failure:* the design deliberately lets a card sit outside the region that owns
it. The tether is the whole of how that stays legible.

**4.2 Drag it back until it straddles the region's edge.** No tether — it's
visibly inside.

**5.2 Delete `.maugham/canvas.json` by hand and reopen.** The layout is gone; the

**6.1 VoiceOver on, walk the canvas.** Regions announce as regions — "Region, Act
II fog, 3 cards" — not just a bare label.

**6.2 Collapse a region and walk again.** Its hidden residents are **not** in the

## Part 7 — judgment calls, routed to your eye

**7.1 Double-clicking a region's label bar mints a scrap on top of the label.**
The obvious gesture for "rename this region" currently makes a stray card. It's
also the documented repro for one of the two new tripwires, so changing it isn't
free. Should double-clicking the bar rename in place instead?

**7.2 A sweep takes a card that already lives in another region.** Resolved by
your 2026-07-28 ruling — creation absorbs, so drawing a box around a card claims
it and `join` moves its home. What's left for your eye is whether *stealing* is
right: the alternative was to skip cards already spoken for, which would leave a
region holding some of what you swept and not the rest, with nothing on screen to
say which. Sweep over a card that lives elsewhere and see whether the theft reads
as obvious or as a surprise.

**⊘ And one mechanical thing while you're there:** a theft mutates **two**
regions, and only the new one ends up selected. If you have the *old* region's
inspector open when the sweep happens, check it actually loses the card.

*(The other half of that ruling — a scrap created inside a region joins it — is
now check 1.2's territory, and the transition half is 1.2b.)*

**7.3 A scrap can only be deleted with ⌫.** A region has a visible Delete button
in its inspector; a scrap has no inspector to put one in. The asymmetry now
*reads* as intentional, which is the wrong signal.

**7.4 Region wash and tether weight, in both appearances.** Dark was measured
(the wash clears the ground by 15 of 255 levels and sits 40% under the
felt-not-seen ceiling), but measured isn't the same as looked at. At low zoom a
tether dash and a region outline both effectively vanish.

**7.5 Inspector responsiveness during a big region drag.** With a region selected
and many cards on the canvas, drag it and watch the right column for jank. Known
and bounded: the inspector's body is rebuilt per frame, though its expensive
lists are not recomputed.

## Known-open, please don't chase

- **The binding feeds nothing yet.** Its consumer is the reference rail beside the
  editor, in an unwritten milestone.
- **No "+N more" chip.** A region too short to show all its reference chips
  silently shows what fits; the inspector always lists them all.
- **The cite menu has no search or cap.** Fine at Playlist scale, wrong at several
  hundred cards.
- **Nested regions are out of scope.** A region swept across another is *not*
  nesting — membership is explicit, so one frame enclosing another means nothing.
