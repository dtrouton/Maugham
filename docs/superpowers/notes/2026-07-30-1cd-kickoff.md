## Seven things that will bite 1C-d specifically

1. **c3 is the first producer of item nodes, and item nodes are half-built by design.** They draw as dashed placeholders carrying a reference id. `CanvasClaudePlacement` sets `CanvasCardMetrics.itemPlaceholderHeight` at creation, and `CanvasView.rebuildLayouts` now has an `.item` heal arm writing that height when `cachedHeight` is nil (added by c3's Critical fix). **The moment a thumbnail makes an item's height depend on its image, that constant stops being the answer** and item nodes need a real measurement pass — the one `rebuildLayouts` deliberately does not give them today.
2. **Resize is `.scrap`-only now, gesture AND mark, and that was a Critical.** Resizing a page card used to delete it permanently: the triangle drew unconditionally, `CanvasInteraction.begin` had no `.scrap` guard, `setWidth` nils `cachedHeight`, nothing re-measured an item node, and a node with no height is neither drawn nor clickable — persisted to the sidecar. **When 1C-d makes an item's width mean something, re-opening this is a deliberate design act**: take the mark and the target together (one constant sizes both, and `drawCard`'s comment calls moving a mark across the conditional line a design change, not a tidy-up), and restore the measurement pass *first*.
3. **`CanvasRenderer.connectHandleRect` still subtracts `resizeHandleSize` on every card**, so a selected page card's connect dot is placed as though a triangle it no longer draws sat below it. Judged cosmetic (the mark and its target still agree with each other, which is the invariant). **1C-d is the slice that makes it visible** if an item node ever gains chrome of its own.
4. **§8A.2's reproduction corollary is STRUCTURAL, not yet visible.** ADR 0026 decision 10 says *"structural here, visible at 1C-d"* in those words. The photograph is on the canvas, in the region, tied to the derived scraps — and it draws as `Item · res-…`, so the writer cannot compare the reading against the page without clicking through. **1C-d's thumbnail is what closes it**, and that is the slice's constitutional obligation rather than a nicety.
5. **The sidecar is at schema 7** (`author` on node, line and region). 1C-d's own field is its own bump to **8**, additive-optional both ways, and **must not fold in anyone else's**. The "from the future" fixture is at **8** (`CanvasLineCodecTests.test_aSchemaEightSidecarLosesTheArrangementAndKeepsTheWords`) and has needed rebumping at *every* bump so far or it stops exercising the refuse-a-newer-schema path — it goes to 9.
6. **Two new guards exist and you must write to them, not around them.** (a) `TripwireGrepTests`' nil-chain census forbids an `XCTAssert*` subject reached through `?.` — unwrap first; the escape is a named `// nil-chain-ok:` marker.
