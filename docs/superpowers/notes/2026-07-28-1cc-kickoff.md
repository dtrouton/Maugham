## Why the draft is stale, and doubly so

1C-c was written against **1C-a's API before it existed and 1C-b's before it existed.** 1C-b in particular shipped a long way from its own draft, and 1C-c's assumptions about the model layer are the part most likely to be quietly wrong:

- The scene, scrap text, selection, the sidecar store and the undo recorder live on an `@Observable **CanvasModel**` owned by `ProjectWindow`, not in `CanvasView`'s `@State`.
- There are **two undo-bracket verbs and they are not interchangeable** (tripwire 32). From inside `CanvasView` a mutation arriving mid-gesture must be **refused**; from another column `mutateFromInspector` **closes, runs and reopens**. A grep census enforces this — a new surface reaching for the wrong one fails the suite.
- `CanvasSelection` is one enum over nodes *and* regions, wired to single-click.
- **The sidecar is already at schema 2** (regions). 1C-c's "schema 2 → 3" heading happens to be right; check everything under it.
- **Creation absorbs** (2026-07-28 ruling, spec §4.2 and ADR 0026 §8 amended in place): a swept region takes the cards whose centres it encloses, and a scrap made inside a region joins it. Move and resize still change nothing. Anything in the draft assuming "membership only ever changes by a drop" is now wrong.
- The routing into each window column goes through **pure functions** (`inspectorRoute`, `editorRoute`) tested exhaustively over segment × project type. A new surface joins there, not in a nested `if`.

**Grep the real source. Never the old plan text.** Every one of those was greppable the moment 1C-b merged.

## What the re-derivation must carry forward

These are what the last two slices actually cost. In priority order:

1. **A green suite cannot tell a fully-exercised function from a *reachable* one.** Three unreachable halves have shipped in this area — 1C-a's ⌘Z (22 tests deep, greyed out in the Edit menu), `CanvasScene.remove` (built, exercised, no caller), `addAppearance` (stored, drawn, listed, removable, uncreatable). **All three were found by counting callers; none by a test.** One `grep` per function a slice adds. `AREA.md` carries the table.
2. **A caller count is not enough on its own.** The region inspector *had* a caller — just not on both branches of a project-type split one level above it, so it was unreachable in Collections, which is what Denver writes in. **Extract the routing decision into a pure function and test it exhaustively over its inputs.**
3. **When a test hand-drives a signal production is supposed to emit, one test must drive the production emitter.** The first whole-branch review found a defect spanning four tasks whose guarding test was individually falsifiable, discriminated both ways, and rested on a premise stated only in its own docstring.
4. **For anything with a menu item, a key equivalent or a gesture, one test must model the real delivery path end to end.** 1C-a shipped a ⌘Z nothing could reach with 22 green undo tests behind it.
5. **An undo test whose only observable is the post-⌘Z scene cannot tell "its own step" from "folded into the neighbour's."** Assert the step's *name* — what the writer reads in the Edit menu.
6. **Nine assertions that could not fail for the reason they existed were found during 1C-b, several in the plan's own test code** — one passing on 8-bit quantisation noise, one on a coincidence where the test's own ⌘Z composed two steps into the right answer. Ask it of every assertion you write. Mutation batteries are the house standard; 1C-b's tasks ran 16–45 each.
7. **Five wrong repros were written into durable places during 1C-b**, one past a review that had already caught the same class. **A repro nobody can reproduce is worse than none** — the next author tries it, fails, and concludes the rule is stale.

## Constraints that will otherwise be rediscovered

- **`ProjectWindow.body` has a zero expression budget.** A `@State` property is free; a body line is not. Column arms must stay one expression.
- **`CanvasView.swift` has five source-layout contracts** in its header comment, enforced by tests that slice the file as *text*. Read them before editing it.
- **The redraw counter and the structural counter are different things**, and `CanvasView`'s structural copy is now written in exactly one place (the mirror off the model). Nothing scene-proportional may key off the redraw counter (tripwire 30).
- Run `xcodebuild` in the **foreground**, one at a time — two invocations contend for one DerivedData. **Never dispatch two implementers at once**; they share the working tree.
- `-only-testing` takes `MaughamTests/<ClassName>`, **never a folder path** (a folder path runs zero tests and reports success).
- `./gen.sh` after adding any file; **never commit anything under `Maugham.xcodeproj/`**.
- SourceKit's `No such module` / `Cannot find type … in scope` is stale-index noise. The one real diagnostic is *"unable to type-check this expression in reasonable time"*.
- **One observation, not yet a rule:** the phone simulator failed three consecutive runs with "Busy / failed preflight checks" and re-running did *not* clear it, contrary to CLAUDE.md. `xcrun simctl boot` on the device the destination actually resolves to fixed it immediately. Watch for a second occurrence before sharpening that line.
