# Two loops P1 — the two verbs

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Author's ⌘R is a *check* and Review's Run round is a *round*, and the two never share a reader, a lane, a round number, a delta rule or an output shape. A check reads the delta, is the coach's (or plain Claude's), files no round, stamps no pass, signs its notes with the reader's name, and shows in Diagnostics. A round reads the piece whole, is a stage editor's, files and numbers as today, and shows in the cockpit and the queue. The persona is read at the keystroke and carried; nothing infers what the writer has declared. One-loop §7.0 is true again.

**Architecture:** a `RunKind` (`check`/`round`) minted from the key window's persona in `CompilerRunModifier` and carried through `runRequested` → `StreamingRun` → `CompilerRun.kind` (tolerated-missing; a legacy record reads its kind off `passId`). Two reader resolutions replace `PieceReader`: `AuthorReader` (coach / nobody — P2 adds the first reader) and `RoundEditor` (the validated stage pass, else nil = no round possible). The orchestrator keys the marker, the round mint, the prior-round briefing, the draft stage and the pass stamp on the kind. `DiagnosticsStore` holds one standing **check** and one standing **round** per document; asks are keyed per kind. The prompt gets a whole-piece section for rounds and two kind-scoped builders. Author's pane and Review's cockpit each lose the other's controls; the board loses its seat row.

**Tech Stack:** Swift/SwiftUI/AppKit; `claude -p`; XCTest via `./scripts/test.sh` (+ `swift test --parallel --package-path Packages/MaughamCore` if Core is touched — this plan expects NOT to touch Core); headless `TestWindow` fixture for mounted tests; tripwire 33 (no press-then-wait) applies to every new test.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-05-two-loops-two-readers-design.md` §4.1–4.2, §4.4–4.9, §5 (P1's half). **Handoff:** `docs/superpowers/notes/2026-09-05-two-loops-handoff.md`. **Out of this plan:** the first reader (§4.3, `firstReaderName`, the statement kind, the reader picker's arms, the four reader kinds, `read_first_reader`) — P2's; the per-tempo help rewrite, the prior specs' cleanup and the register sweep — P3's.

**Branch:** `claude/two-loops-p1-2026-09-05` off local `main` after `claude/compiler-author-review-split-x5tv61` (the spec, this plan, the handoff) is merged into it by fast-forward.
