# Two loops P2 — the first reader

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Author gains a second reader beside the coach — **one first reader per project**, named by the writer, described in a project-scope statement of her own, chosen from a picker in the Diagnostics header, briefed with that description and her standing instructions, and answering only as a reader: what she now believes, where the dream broke, where she got bored, where she got lost, and a short letter with no craft verdict in it. Her notes are signed with her name and unstamped. Claude can read her definition (`read_first_reader`) and never write it.

**Architecture:** the name is identity and lives on `ProjectManifest.firstReaderName` (MaughamCore, additive, tolerated-missing); the description is a `Statement.Kind.firstReader` (`first-reader.md`, project scope, hosted by `StatementPane` like every statement, written only by the writer and `RulingPerformer`); the writer's CHOICE of reader is UI state (`UIState.authorReaderChoice`), defaulting to the coach while her seat is held, else the first reader if named, else nobody. `AuthorReader` grows `.firstReader(FirstReader)` and the check's briefing gets the `readerSection` the spec names (§4.9) — the coach's teacher frame moves there from `passSection`, so `ActivePass.isCoach` dies and a check no longer produces an `ActivePass` at all. `Environment.reader(docId, kind)` splits into `authorReader(docId)` and `roundEditor(docId)`. The `reader` section's vocabulary widens to four kinds at the schema, the parser and the label. A first reader's letter is a third `LetterDosage` (`.reader`: answer/about/working/≤1 question, nothing else) enforced at ingest and stated in the briefing. `CompilerRun.readerName` is stamped on every check so Author's standing letter, Keep and provenance sign with who actually read (P1's Ruling 10 carry).

**Tech Stack:** Swift/SwiftUI/AppKit; `claude -p`; XCTest via `./scripts/test.sh` **plus `swift test --parallel --package-path Packages/MaughamCore`** (Tasks 1 and 3 touch Core — run both schemes); headless `TestWindow` fixture; tripwire 33 (no press-then-wait) on every new test.
