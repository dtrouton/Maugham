# Editorial letter P2 — the ask and the ledger

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** the writer can say what they want this round's feedback *on* and get it answered first; the project keeps a ledger of what the writer has learned and what they have decided is a choice, in the writer's own prose, moved only by the writer's verbs; every round is briefed on that ledger, cites a known habit by its heading instead of rediscovering it, never raises a choice, and — on a cold read — offers to retire a lesson it looked for and did not find; and an outside Claude can read the ledger.

**Architecture:** the ask is per document, per device, in the diagnostics directory (a small file beside the refused-cold-start record, because the run sidecar cannot exist before the first run), read by the orchestrator at the keystroke and briefed as its own per-run section OUTSIDE the briefing hash; `answer` is one more optional key on the `letter` line. The ledger is a fourth `Statement.Kind`, `.lessons`, project scope only, hosted by the existing `StatementPane`/`StatementEditorHost` with a `DetailSegment` case in Author and Review; its entries are ordinary rulings under `## Rulings` read through one pure grammar (`LessonsLedger`: open lesson / choice / retired lesson), briefed INSIDE the hash by a `lessonsSection`, and written only through `RulingPerformer` by four writer verbs that live in ONE file. A minted letter question carries the habit heading it was raised under, threaded onto `Op.Provenance` additively, so the queue can offer *This is a choice* and the second-stet offer with exact identity. `read_lessons` is a fourth spine reader on `read_craft_intent`'s shape.

**Tech Stack:** Swift/SwiftUI/AppKit; `claude -p`; XCTest via `./scripts/test.sh` (+ `swift test --parallel --package-path Packages/MaughamCore` after touching Core); headless `TestWindow` fixture for mounted tests.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-29-the-editorial-letter-design.md` §3.7 (the ask) and §6 (the ledger; revised 2026-09-01: retire and choice are writer-confirmed offers, never automatic). **Handoff:** `docs/superpowers/notes/2026-09-02-editorial-letter-handoff.md` — its "P2" section settles the shape below; do not re-derive it. **Out of this plan:** §5/§3.8 process and dosage (`process`, the draft stage, the lane line's stage word) — P3's.
